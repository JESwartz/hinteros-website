The browser demo is not available yet. Install HinterOS, or try it in a VM with nix run git+{{forge}}/hinteros.git#vm.

Controls

Use a keyboard or a controller. The controller operates after its first button push. Click the screen before you use the keyboard. Touch and mouse input do not operate on the screen. To use them, push Show controller.

Move
D-pad or left stick; arrow keys
Open or select
A; Enter
Back
B; Backspace
Close an app or game
Y; Y
Previous or next tab
LB or RB; Page Up or Page Down
Go to Home
View or Guide; Home (on many laptops Fn+←)

Apps and games do not open in the demo. They show a placeholder screen. To go back to Home, push B, Backspace or Home. The app or game then shows under Continue. A page reload resets the demo.
